Our Story

Est. 2010

A Humble Beginning, a Sweet Legacy

Sweet Dreams was born in the autumn of 2010 in a small rented kitchen on Maple Street, when founder Elena Rossi decided to turn her lifelong passion for patisserie into something the whole neighbourhood could enjoy. Armed with her grandmother's recipe book, a single stand mixer, and an unwavering belief that desserts should be made with honest ingredients, Elena opened the doors to what was then a modest counter-service café.

Word spread quickly. Within a year, the weekend queues stretched around the block, and by 2013 the shop had expanded into the adjoining premises, adding a full production kitchen and a cosy seating area decorated with vintage baking tins and hand-lettered menus. Today, Sweet Dreams serves hundreds of guests every day — yet every croissant is still laminated by hand, every tart shell still blind-baked to order, and every celebration cake still decorated with the same care that Elena poured into that very first batch of almond financiers.

Our Philosophy

Crafted by Hand. Inspired by Nature.

We believe that truly exceptional pastry begins long before it reaches the oven — it starts with the choices made at the market, the farm, and the mill.

🌿

Premium Ingredients

Every ingredient we use is sourced from trusted local farms and specialist suppliers. Our butter comes from grass-fed cows, our flour is stone-milled, and our seasonal fruits are picked at peak ripeness to ensure the deepest, most natural flavours in every bite.

🕐

Handcrafted Daily

Nothing at Sweet Dreams is pre-made or mass-produced. Our pastry chefs arrive before dawn each morning to prepare fresh doughs, custards, and glazes from scratch. This dedication to craftsmanship means our display case changes with the seasons — and with our chefs' creativity.

❤️

Community First

Sweet Dreams has always been more than a bakery — it is a gathering place. We partner with local schools for baking workshops, donate surplus pastries to community shelters every evening, and celebrate neighbourhood milestones with custom cakes baked with genuine affection.

The People

Meet the Hands Behind the Magic

Our small but passionate team brings together decades of patisserie experience, a love of local produce, and an unshakeable commitment to making your day a little sweeter.

Elena Rossi

Founder & Head Pastry Chef

Trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris and with 20 years of patisserie experience, Elena's philosophy is simple: use the best ingredients, respect the craft, and always bake with joy.

Marco Bianchi

Head Baker

Marco's mastery of fermentation and laminated doughs has earned Sweet Dreams a reputation for the finest croissants in the city. He sources his heritage grains directly from a family mill in Tuscany.

Sophie Laurent

Cake Designer

With a background in fine arts and sugar craft, Sophie transforms every celebration cake into an edible sculpture. Her botanical designs and hand-painted tiers have been featured in several bridal publications.
